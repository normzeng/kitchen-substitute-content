  • Lemon juice
    • Lime juice — per 1 Tbsp lemon juice — 1:1 — the same amount of lime juice — 1:1
      What changes: Greener aroma
    • White vinegar — per 1 Tbsp lemon juice — White vinegar, 1:1 in cooking; use 3/4 the amount in dressings — 1:1
      What changes: Sharper, no citrus notes
  • Buttermilk
    • Milk + lemon juice or vinegar — per 1 cup buttermilk — 1 Tbsp lemon juice or white vinegar + enough milk to reach 1 cup; rest 10 min — 240 ml milk + 15 ml acid
      What changes: Slightly thinner than real buttermilk

      What changes: Slightly thicker result
    • Milk + cream of tartar — per 1 cup buttermilk — 1 3/4 tsp cream of tartar + enough milk to reach 1 cup — 8 g cream of tartar + 240 ml milk
  • Heavy cream
    • Milk + melted butter — per 1 cup heavy cream — 3/4 cup milk + 1/4 cup melted butter — 180 ml milk + 60 g melted butter
      What changes: Will not whip
    • Half-and-half + butter — per 1 cup heavy cream — 7/8 cup half-and-half + 1/8 cup melted butter — 210 ml half-and-half + 30 g butter
      What changes: Will not whip
