Questions

Fudgy or cakey — which swap?
Flax eggs bind without leavening: denser, fudgier. For cakey brownies use aquafaba instead — 3 Tbsp per egg, whipped until foamy before folding.
Do I need to change the bake time?
No. The flax version may look slightly less set at the usual time; the toothpick test is the same.
