Egg-free

Brownies without eggs

Choose the texture first. The swap follows from it.

Eggs in a brownie are a texture decision disguised as an ingredient. Replace them with flax and you get fudgy. Replace them with aquafaba and you get closer to cakey. The sheet below defaults to fudgy, because they're brownies.

Butter and chocolate stay. Nothing else in a brownie involves eggs.

The egg-free pan

semisweet chocolate1 cup
butter1/2 cup
Flax eggs
2 Tbsp ground flaxseed + 6 Tbsp water
granulated sugar1 cup
all-purpose flour3/4 cup
cocoa powder1/3 cup
salt1/4 tsp

One marked line changes, and it decides the whole texture.

eggs

2 Tbsp ground flaxseed + 6 Tbsp water

Two flax eggs, gelled and stirred in like eggs. The batter is thick enough that the binding barely notices, and the fudge factor goes up.

Fudgier and slightly denser. That's the point of this route.

The other route

I actually want them cakey.
Aquafaba: 3 Tbsp per egg, whipped until foamy before folding in. It carries air the way whites do and the crumb comes out lighter.
Do they underbake?
The toothpick test is unchanged. The flax version looks less set straight out of the oven and firms as it cools, so trust the timer the first time.
