Common questions

Can I substitute milk for buttermilk in Thanksgiving baking?
Not straight — buttermilk's acid activates baking soda. Per cup: 1 Tbsp lemon juice or vinegar + enough milk to reach 1 cup, rest 10 minutes. The full buttermilk page has every option.
What's the fastest gravy fix if I'm out of cornstarch?
All-purpose flour: twice the amount, cooked out as a slurry or roux. Exact amounts on the flour for cornstarch page.
