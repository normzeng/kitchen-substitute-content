Questions

Do the flax eggs change the cookie?
They bind but don't leaven, so the cookie bakes slightly denser and chewier. For chocolate chip cookies that's a trade many people actively prefer.
Which plant butter for creaming?
Stick-style, straight from the fridge. Tub spreads have more water and the dough goes cakey.
