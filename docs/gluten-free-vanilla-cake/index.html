Questions

Why must the blend contain xanthan gum?
Gluten is the cake's structure; xanthan gum is what replaces it in the blend. A gum-free blend in a cake built for wheat flour collapses.
What changes in the crumb?
More fragile and slightly denser than wheat, and browning runs a touch different. Same flavor, same moisture.
