Vegan pumpkin bread

Pumpkin purée is already an egg replacer. This recipe just admits it.

Quick breads forgive vegan swaps better than almost anything else in the oven. The purée holds the moisture and the leaveners do the lifting, so the animal products were only ever bringing fat and binding. Both are straightforward to replace.

Three marked lines change. The pumpkin was vegan the whole time.

eggs

2 Tbsp ground flaxseed + 6 Tbsp water

Two flax eggs, gelled. In pumpkin bread the binding matters more than lift, which makes flax the right tool here rather than a compromise.

Denser, moister crumb. The pumpkin carries it.

butter

1/2 cup

Half a cup of stick plant butter, softened or melted depending on how the recipe builds the batter. Either behaves.

Check the brand's salt level before adding the recipe's salt.

milk

1/2 cup

Half a cup of unsweetened soy or oat, whatever's open. Liquid duty, nothing more.

Worth knowing

Aquafaba instead?
You can (3 Tbsp per egg, whipped to a foam), but its talent is whipping and a loaf doesn't whip. Flax fits this bake better.
Does the flax really need the ten minutes?
Yes. Ungelled flax is seed dust in water; the rest is what turns it into a binder.
