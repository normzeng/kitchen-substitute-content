Common questions

What can I use instead of Sesame oil?
Neutral oil: 1 Tbsp. Toast neutral oil with sesame seeds: Warm 3 Tbsp neutral oil + 1 Tbsp sesame seeds 5 min; strain.
What is the exact ratio to substitute Sesame oil?
Neutral oil (per 1 Tbsp sesame oil) — US: 1 Tbsp. Metric: 15 ml.
What changes when you substitute Sesame oil?
Lighter aroma. Loses the nutty aroma.
