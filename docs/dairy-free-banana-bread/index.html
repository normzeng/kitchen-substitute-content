Dairy-free

Banana bread without dairy

The bananas were doing the important work all along.

Dairy-free baking sounds like a project until you look at what dairy actually does in a banana bread: some fat in the butter, a splash of liquid from the milk. The fruit handles the moisture. Two measured swaps and it's the same loaf.

Eggs stay in this version, because dairy-free and egg-free are different problems. If you need both, the FAQ covers the egg.

The dairy-free loaf

ripe bananas3 medium
Stick plant butter
1/2 cup
Unsweetened soy or oat milk
1/4 cup
egg1 egg
granulated sugar3/4 cup
all-purpose flour1 3/4 cups
baking soda1 tsp

Only the marked lines change; the bananas couldn't care less what you pour in.

butter

1/2 cup

Half a cup of stick plant butter, softened. It creams into the sugar the same way dairy butter does and bakes the same crumb. Use a stick, not a tub, for the usual reason: tubs carry more water.

Salt varies by brand, though with this much banana you may never notice.

milk

1/4 cup

A quarter cup of unsweetened soy or oat. This exists in the recipe purely as liquid, and banana flavor steamrolls any plant-milk note you've ever worried about.

Soy sits marginally closer to dairy milk in the crumb; oat works fine.

Before you bake

What if it needs to be vegan as well?
Swap the egg for a flax egg: 1 Tbsp ground flaxseed + 3 Tbsp water, rested 10 minutes until gelled. Denser, moister crumb, still very much banana bread.
Does it brown the same?
Plant butter browns a touch faster on top in some ovens. Peek five minutes early the first time.
