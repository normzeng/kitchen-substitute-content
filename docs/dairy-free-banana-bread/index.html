Questions

Which plant milk for banana bread?
Unsweetened soy or oat, 1:1. Banana flavor covers any plant-milk note completely.
Can I make it vegan too?
Yes — the egg swap is the flax egg: 1 Tbsp ground flaxseed + 3 Tbsp water, rested 10 minutes until gelled. Denser, moister crumb.
