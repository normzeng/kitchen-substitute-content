# Privacy Policy — Kitchen Substitute

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Kitchen Substitute processes recipes on your device. It has no developer-operated recipe
storage or app-operated cloud sync, and does not automatically send recipes or photos to
the developer.

- **No accounts.** The app works without sign-up.
- **No analytics, tracking or advertising.** The app has no analytics or advertising SDKs.
- **Recipe photos.** Camera and imported images are read with Apple Vision on your device.
  Images are used temporarily for text recognition, then discarded by the app; image copies
  are not saved or uploaded. Original photos in your Photos library are unaffected. The
  system photo picker provides only images you select and may download an image from
  iCloud Photos if it is not already available locally.
- **Saved recipes.** Saving stores the title, original or recognized text, ingredients,
  directions, dietary options, cooking notes, earlier conversion references and folders in
  local app storage. Unit preferences are also stored locally.
- **Sharing and support.** Sharing happens when you choose the system share action and a
  destination. The chosen app or recipient receives the recipe text you share. If you email
  support, the developer receives the information you include in that email.
- **Deletion and backups.** Delete a recipe in Recipes to remove its saved data from the
  local library. Deleting a folder keeps its recipes, unfiled. Deleting the app removes its
  local recipe library. Normal iOS iCloud or computer backups may include saved recipes,
  depending on your backup settings; existing backups and previously shared copies are
  managed separately.
- **Content updates.** The app checks for signed substitution-content updates from this
  repository's public releases. GitHub receives standard network-request information,
  such as your IP address; these requests do not include recipes or photos. Opening support
  or privacy links, downloading an iCloud photo, or sharing uses the relevant system service
  or destination's network and privacy practices.

## Data collected
The app does not collect recipe photos, recipe text or dietary options on developer servers,
and does not collect analytics or tracking data. Local processing and storage are separate
from information you intentionally share or send to support, as described above.
