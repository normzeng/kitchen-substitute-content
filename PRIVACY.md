# Privacy Policy — Kitchen Substitute

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Kitchen Substitute does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data.

- **No accounts.** The app works without sign-up.
- **No analytics or tracking.** No third-party SDKs, no identifiers, no advertising.
- **No network access except content updates.** The only network activity is a periodic
  check for updated substitution content, downloaded from this repository's public releases.
  That check transmits nothing about you or your device beyond the standard HTTP request.
- **No user content leaves the device.** Searches, filters, and unit preferences are stored
  locally on your device only.

## Data collected
None.
