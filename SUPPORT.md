# Support — Kitchen Substitute

Contact: **aechopp@gmail.com**

Include your device model and iOS version where possible.

Common questions:

- **The app is missing an ingredient.** Use "Request this ingredient" shown after an empty
  search, or email us — requests go into the next content update, delivered automatically
  in-app.
- **A ratio looks wrong.** Email us with the entry name; verified corrections ship in the
  next content update, usually the same day.
- **Do I need an account?** No. The app is fully offline and collects nothing.
- **How do I cancel a subscription?** There isn't one — it is a one-time purchase.
