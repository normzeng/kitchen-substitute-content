# Support — Kitchen Substitute

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Contact: **aechopp@gmail.com**

Include your device model and iOS version where possible.

Common questions:

- **How do I add a recipe?** On Home, tap "Convert a recipe." Choose "Take a recipe photo"
  or "Import a recipe photo," or type ingredients and directions yourself. You can add
  another photo for additional pages. Photos selected inside the app are read on your
  device and discarded after recognition; the recognized text remains editable.
- **Can I paste a link or share from another app?** On Home, tap "Import a recipe" to
  paste a public recipe link, recipe text or an image. You can also choose Kitchen
  Substitute in another app's Share menu and tap "Add to imports," then open Kitchen
  Substitute to review and save it. Shared content waits in a local inbox; it is not
  automatically filed in your recipe library.
- **A website will not import.** Some sites block direct requests or do not provide a
  usable recipe. If "Open in Safari" is offered, open the page, tap the import button,
  choose "Add to imports," then tap Done in the extension and browser to return to the
  recipe review. You can also paste the recipe text or import a screenshot. Check the
  title, ingredient list and directions against the source before saving.
- **The recognized text is wrong.** Try a clear, well-lit photo. Correct the Ingredients
  text under "Edit ingredient text," keeping one ingredient per line, and put cooking
  steps in Directions. Apple Intelligence can help read ingredient names on supported
  devices when available; otherwise use the app's recognition and manual editing. Reading
  a food name is separate from finding a suitable dietary swap.
- **How do dietary swaps work?** Choose an eating style (vegan, vegetarian, pescatarian or
  no preference) and any additional ingredients to avoid. Check the replacements shown
  beside their originals, amounts, preparation notes and adapted numbered directions, then
  tap "Save." You can choose another available swap or keep the original when appropriate.
  Unidentified foods, unsupported cooking roles and label questions remain marked for
  review. Not every recipe can be converted. Keeping an incompatible original does not
  make it meet the selected diet. Check product labels and cross-contact risks; suggestions
  do not certify that a product or finished dish is safe for an allergy.
- **I changed the recipe or dietary options.** Check the updated ingredients and directions
  before saving. Changes are applied together when you tap "Save." Original text and
  conversion references remain available; use the current reviewed amounts and method.
- **Will an update change recipes I already saved?** Opening a saved recipe does not
  silently replace its ingredients or directions. Choose "Review updated replacements"
  when offered, review the changes, then Save to adopt them. If an older import omitted
  ingredients or steps, reimport the source and check it before saving; the app cannot
  reconstruct missing source text. Removing dietary choices restores the stored source
  ingredients and directions for a saved conversion.
- **How do I save and reopen a recipe?** Give it a title, choose or create a folder, and tap
  "Save." Open "Recipes" from Home to find it again, edit it or share its text. Recipes and
  folders are saved locally; there is no app-operated cloud sync.
- **Camera access was denied.** Allow camera access for Kitchen Substitute in iOS Settings
  if you want to take a photo. You can also use "Import a recipe photo" or enter text manually.
- **Can I use the app offline?** Ingredient lookup, calculations, recipe recognition and
  saving work offline with bundled content and locally available images. Apple Intelligence
  assistance depends on device support and model availability. Website imports, images
  stored only in iCloud Photos and content updates require internet access.
- **How long do shared imports stay on my phone?** The temporary inbox supports up to
  five images per import, at most 8 MiB each and 20 MiB total. Inbox content is removed
  after a successful save or explicit discard. Items older than seven days are removed
  when the inbox is next used, rather than continuously in the background. Saved recipes
  remain in the library. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md) for storage and backup details.
- **How do I delete or keep a copy of a recipe?** Delete it from the Recipes list to remove
  it from the local library. Deleting a folder leaves its recipes unfiled. Use the recipe's
  Share action to keep a text copy before deleting the app, which removes the local library.
  Saved recipes may also be included in iOS iCloud or computer backups according to your
  backup settings. Existing backups and shared copies are managed separately.
- **The app is missing an ingredient.** Use the request link shown after an empty search,
  or email us with the ingredient name so it can be reviewed for a future content update.
- **A ratio looks wrong.** Email us with the entry name and the issue so it can be reviewed.
- **Do I need an account?** No. Photo processing and saved recipes work without sign-up.
- **How do I cancel a subscription?** There isn't one — it is a one-time purchase.
